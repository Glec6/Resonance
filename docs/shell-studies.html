STUDY A

Orbit

The library is a ring of discs in space. The selected disc swings to the front, flattens toward you and spins up; the room takes on that game's colours. Starting a game drops the disc into the drive with a light ring.

STARTING

What moves

  • Discs ride a spring, so a fast flick overshoots and settles
  • A light wedge sweeps the disc surface as it spins
  • Background light fields fade to the new game's palette in 0.6 s
  • Title letters rise in one by one

On the PS4

One Vulkan pass in the launcher process: discs are textured quads with a rotating highlight, the background is three soft blobs. Box art can replace the printed label once covers download.

STUDY B

Memory unit

A big monochrome dot-matrix screen, built like the little memory cards that plugged into Dreamcast pads. Every game gets a pixel emblem, a scrolling marquee and a slow LCD ghost when the picture changes. The library list slides beside it.

What moves

  • Pixels dissolve in random order between games
  • Each dot fades at LCD speed, leaving a short ghost
  • Emblems breathe between two frames, the title scrolls
  • The list highlight springs to the selection

On the PS4

48 × 32 cells drawn as one instanced quad batch; the emblem and text are computed on the CPU, a few kilobytes per frame. The cheapest of the three, and it could also show real save icons from the memory card files.

STUDY C

Channel

Every game is a TV channel. Switching tunes through static and a vertical roll, and a broadcast lower third slides in. The picture behind it is an attract scene tinted in the game's colours, seen through scanlines. Starting a game switches the set off and on again.

What moves

  • Static burst and vertical roll on every channel change
  • Red and blue fringes split the title for a moment
  • A low-resolution attract scene scrolls under scanlines
  • Power-off collapses the picture to a line and a dot

On the PS4

The scene renders at 192 × 108 into an off-screen image and is scaled up with a scanline shader, so it costs almost nothing. The static is a noise texture shifted each frame.
